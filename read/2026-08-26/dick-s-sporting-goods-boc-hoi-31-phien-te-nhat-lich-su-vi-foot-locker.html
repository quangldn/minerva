Dick's Falls Most Ever With Chairman Saying More Pain to Come

Bloomberg · 2026-08-25

Dick's Sporting Goods Inc. sank the most on record after reporting that the Foot Locker chain it acquired last year continues to struggle, raising doubts about the broader sneaker market.

The company now expects net sales to be in a range of $21.9 billion to $22.2 billion in the current fiscal year, down from its previous forecast. The change in outlook was driven by a drop in sales at Foot Locker, which Dick's bought last year for $2.4 billion.
