Russia Eyeing Sabotage, Other Attacks in Europe, Officials Warn

Russia may escalate attacks against the Baltic States and Poland, a European intelligence official said, amid rising concern that Moscow wants to further intimidate nations supporting Ukraine. Russian intelligence and security services are looking at hybrid warfare operations, including infrastructure sabotage or false-flag attacks.

Separately, the US Central Intelligence Agency told countries in the region about the potential of a future Russian attack. "The biggest concern right now is a series of sabotage operations," said Seth Jones of the Center for Strategic and International Studies.

The warnings come after Germany's Leipzig-Halle airport, a major NATO military logistics hub, temporarily suspended flights last week after an armed drone was found next to a Ukrainian aircraft.
