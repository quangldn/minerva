Ukraine hits grain terminals at Russia's Novorossiysk port, killing three

The Russian Black Sea city 'endured a horrific night', with a child killed and dozens of buildings damaged, says mayor. Published On 12 Aug 2026 (Al Jazeera, with Reuters).

Ukraine has pounded Russia's Black Sea port city of Novorossiysk in a massive overnight drone attack that killed three people and put two major grain terminals out of action, according to local officials, raising fears in global markets of a disruption to supplies.

A child was among those killed in the Ukrainian attacks on Wednesday, according to regional Governor Veniamin Kondratyev. At least 24 people were wounded.

Ukraine's SBU security service said the attack had "strategic significance" and that Ukrainian forces hit the Novorossiysk naval base with drones and missiles. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said two frigates, a large landing ship, a corvette and other vessels were hit.

Russia is the world's largest wheat exporter, with most of the exports shipped via its Black Sea ports. Russia's main grain lobby group warned last month that Ukrainian attacks could shut down grain exports via the Black Sea in the near future, pushing up prices and causing hunger in Africa and the Middle East.

Novorossiysk Mayor Andrei Kravchenko said the port suspended its water supply and declared an emergency after the strikes on Wednesday. Four enterprises, more than two dozen residential buildings and some educational facilities were damaged, he said. "Novorossiysk endured a horrific night," he wrote in a post on Telegram.

Russia relocated most of its naval ships to Novorossiysk after Ukrainian strikes on the main Black Sea base at Sevastopol on the Crimean Peninsula, which Moscow annexed from Ukraine in 2014, earlier in the more than four-year war. Russia's Ministry of Defence said it downed and destroyed 502 Ukrainian drones overnight.

In Ukraine's Kherson region, a Russian attack using Shahed drones killed two people and injured at least two others. In Zaporizhzhia, a Russian attack triggered a blaze at a shopping mall and cut off power for some 1,200 consumers. A separate Russian attack on Odesa, Ukraine's largest seaport, damaged infrastructure there.

According to a Financial Times report citing Ukrainian officials, Kyiv has also committed to halting drone strikes on oil tankers using the Novorossiysk port after a request at the end of last month from United States Vice President JD Vance. Talks to end Russia's war on Ukraine have largely stalled since the US and Israel launched their war on Iran in late February.
