UN chief: DRC's biggest Ebola outbreak to run out of money in weeks, not months

AFP, via News24. Published 27 August 2026.

UN chief Antonio Guterres appealed Thursday for $1.1 billion to fully fund an aid plan for combating the Ebola epidemic in the Democratic Republic of Congo.

The plan, costing $2.1 billion in total, has received a major contribution from the United States but "without additional funding, vital operations will run out of money, just when they need to expand," he said.

The programme is 48% funded and "current resources will last weeks, not months," Guterres added.

"That is why I am appealing for the remaining $1.1 billion..., recognizing that even more will be needed in the future."

There have been more than 5 600 confirmed Ebola cases in the DRC, including more than 2 700 deaths, since the outbreak was declared on 15 May.

Driven by the rare Bundibugyo species of the virus – for which there are no vaccines or treatments – the outbreak is the biggest in the DRC's history.
