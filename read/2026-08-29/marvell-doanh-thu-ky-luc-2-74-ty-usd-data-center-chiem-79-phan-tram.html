Marvell Q2 Revenue Hits Record $2.74B as Data Center Reaches 79% of Sales

Jim Carroll, Converge Digest — August 27, 2026

Marvell reported record Q2 FY2027 revenue of $2.739 billion, up 37%, as data center sales surged 46% to $2.172 billion and reached 79% of company revenue. Q3 revenue guidance rises to $3.15 billion at the midpoint.

Marvell Technology posted record revenue of $2.739 billion for its fiscal second quarter ended August 1, 2026, up 37% year over year and 13% sequentially as AI infrastructure continued to reshape the company's revenue mix. Data center revenue climbed to $2.172 billion, rising 46% year over year and 18% sequentially, and accounted for 79% of total sales compared with 74% a year earlier. Communications and other revenue reached $567.8 million, up 10% year over year but down 3% from the preceding quarter. CEO Matt Murphy said AI-related bookings remain exceptionally strong, with demand broadening across Marvell's connectivity portfolio and the company expecting a significant acceleration in its custom silicon business during the second half of fiscal 2027.

Profitability also expanded as revenue scaled. GAAP gross margin reached 53.1%, compared with 50.4% a year earlier, while non-GAAP gross margin was 58.9%. GAAP operating income increased to $459.7 million, producing a 16.8% operating margin, while non-GAAP operating income crossed the $1 billion mark at $1.003 billion and non-GAAP operating margin rose to 36.6%. GAAP net income was $308.0 million, or $0.33 per diluted share, while non-GAAP net income reached $865.9 million, or $0.94 per diluted share. Operating cash flow totaled $605.5 million. Marvell also increased R&D spending to $741.1 million from $519.0 million a year earlier as it expands its portfolio across custom compute, networking, optical interconnect, memory connectivity and other data center silicon.

Marvell expects another sizable sequential increase in the third quarter, forecasting revenue of $3.150 billion, plus or minus 5%, which at the midpoint would represent approximately 15% growth from Q2. The company expects non-GAAP diluted EPS of $1.10, plus or minus $0.05, and non-GAAP gross margin of 57.5% to 58.5%. Murphy said Marvell is again raising its revenue outlook for both fiscal 2027 and fiscal 2028 compared with guidance issued last quarter. Marvell plans to provide a broader view of its long-term AI infrastructure strategy at its Investor Day in New York on October 6, 2026.

"AI-related bookings remain exceptionally robust, and we expect our revenue growth to accelerate further through the remainder of fiscal 2027," said Matt Murphy, Marvell Chairman and CEO.

Key AI infrastructure takeaways from the investor call

FY2028 raised again: Marvell now expects approximately $18B of FY2028 revenue, up $1.5B from its outlook just one quarter ago. Management expects roughly 50% YoY company growth, with data center revenue growing more than 60%.

1.6T ramp: 800G optical DSP demand remains strong, but management said the 1.6T business is ramping rapidly and expects that transition to accelerate further in FY2028.

51.2T switching: Marvell expects its Scale Out switching business to more than double this year, driven by the ramp of 51.2T switch silicon across a widening customer base.

Scale Across: As AI clusters extend across multiple data centers, Marvell projects aggregate bandwidth requirements for Scale Across networks at more than 10x current front-end DCI networks. It is targeting this with coherent DSP-enabled 1.6T ZR/ZR+ DCI modules.

Scale Up optics: Management said the Scale Up optics opportunity has increased meaningfully since last quarter. Customers are planning optical Scale Up deployments as early as next year, although Marvell expects copper and optics to coexist for several years.

NPO + CPO: Marvell does not see Scale Up optics as a single-form-factor transition. Customers are evaluating NPO and CPO plus MZM, EAM and MRM modulation technologies. Management emphasized an "and, not an or" architecture.

Supply commitments: To secure semiconductor capacity amid industry constraints, Marvell remains on pace for approximately $1B of supplier capacity prepayments in FY2027.

Analysis

The most important structural metric in Marvell's results is not simply the 37% overall growth rate but the continued shift of the company toward data center infrastructure. Data center revenue moved from 73%-76% of total revenue through much of the preceding two years to 79% in Q2 FY2027.

That shift coincides with a much broader data center portfolio. Marvell closed the Celestial AI acquisition on February 2, adding Photonic Fabric technology for optical Scale Up connectivity, and acquired XConn Technologies on February 10 to expand PCIe, CXL and UALink-related switching capabilities. Marvell also entered a strategic partnership with NVIDIA in March under which NVIDIA invested $2 billion and Marvell is developing custom XPUs and compatible Scale Up networking for the NVLink Fusion ecosystem.
