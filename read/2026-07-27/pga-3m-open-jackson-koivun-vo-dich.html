Jackson Koivun, tay golf 21 tuoi nguoi My, da gianh chuc vo dich PGA Tour dau tien trong su nghiep tai giai 3M Open 2026 tren san TPC Twin Cities (Blaine, Minnesota) ngay 26/7/2026.

Koivun cham dich voi tong diem 259 gay (-25), lap ky luc moi cua giai va bo xa tay golf so 1 the gioi Scottie Scheffler toi 3 gay. Scheffler (262 gay, -22) mot lan nua ve nhi, tren nhom bon nguoi dong hang ba gom Hideki Matsuyama, Brian Harman, Chandler Phillips va Denny McCarthy.

Nha vo dich tre nhan khoan tien thuong 1.584.000 USD trong tong quy thuong 8,8 trieu USD, cong them 500 diem FedEx Cup va 42 diem tren bang xep hang golf the gioi.
