Lockheed Martin and friends demo 5G drone sensing

Lockheed Martin, Verizon, Nvidia, Keysight Technologies, ODC and Astris AI teamed up for a demonstration that's supposed to show how detecting unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) can be done more easily.

The NetSense Airspace Awareness-as-a-Service system used AI to identify, track and monitor UAS by tapping into existing 5G networks. It took place in a critical, high-traffic, urban area somewhere around Miami using Verizon's 5G network spectrum, ODC's AI-native RAN software, Nvidia's AI Aerial platform, and Keysight's RF simulation platform.

"By applying AI to the RF signal measurement data already traversing our 5G infrastructure, we can effectively turn our network into an intelligent sensing platform," said Anil Guntupali, SVP of Technology and Product Development at Verizon.

NetSense will be available as a subscription, with pilot deployments planned for the second half of 2026 and early 2027, and commercial general availability planned for 2027.
