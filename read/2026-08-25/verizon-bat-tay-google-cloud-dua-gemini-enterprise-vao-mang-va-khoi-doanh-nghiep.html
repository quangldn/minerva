Verizon taps into Google Cloud to bring AI to the enterprise

Google Cloud today announced a new strategic partnership agreement with Verizon focused on delivering faster, more intelligent, and highly responsive experiences to consumers and businesses nationwide. By deploying Google Cloud's full-stack AI—including its advanced data infrastructure and Gemini Enterprise—Verizon will continue modernizing its customer experiences, unifying enterprise data, and scaling AI across the enterprise.

Verizon's customer-first strategy includes building a customer-first digital experience supported by Gemini's conversational, multimodal capabilities. This serves as a key tool within Verizon's AI-first toolbox for its customers.

Verizon's longstanding partnership with Google Cloud contact center technology has recently evolved into Gemini Enterprise for Customer Experience, delivering measurable improvements in customer satisfaction and automated resolution rates across digital touchpoints. The platform handles the majority of its inbound consumer calls and chats a month, freeing customer care representatives to dedicate their focus to complex, high-touch customer needs.

Verizon is building an autonomous network intelligence framework, with Google Cloud as a data platform partner to predict and resolve network anomalies before they affect customers. Combined with Verizon's nationwide connectivity, this full-stack integration creates a responsive foundation for next-generation digital services.

Verizon is also leveraging Google Cloud's data and AI solutions to modernize its marketing platforms, automating content creation and campaign orchestration to drive customer engagement, sales, and retention. In parallel, Verizon is strengthening its cloud security posture with advanced threat detection and proactive risk governance.

Beyond network operations and marketing initiatives, Verizon will use Gemini Enterprise for agent orchestration and employee productivity to improve the operating model across their core business functions.
