AST revenues surge but losses mount due to rocket mishap

Revenue came in at $31.5 million, up from $1.2 million in Q2 last year, which AST attributed to gateway deliveries and meeting milestones pertaining to contracts with the US government.

AST's revenue backlog now stands at $1.3 billion. AST said it has now partnered with more than 60 mobile operators that collectively cover 3 billion subscribers.

AST had intended to have more satellites in orbit by now, but a launch failure in April threw a spanner in the works. The launch of commercial services has been delayed until 2027.

Operating expenses increased substantially year-on-year to $329.1 million from $73.95 million, and pre-tax losses widened to $298.8 million from $99.4 million.
