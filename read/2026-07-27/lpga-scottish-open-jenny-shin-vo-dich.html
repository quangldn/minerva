Jenny Shin (Han Quoc) da gianh chuc vo dich giai golf nu ISPS HANDA Women's Scottish Open ngay 26/7/2026 tai san Dundonald Links, danh dau danh hieu LPGA Tour dau tien cua co sau tron mot thap ky.

Buoc vao vong cuoi voi loi the 5 gay, Shin choi chap chung voi diem 75 (+3), nhung van ket thuc tren dinh bang xep hang o muc 9 gay duoi par, hon nguoi dong huong A-Lim Kim (68 gay, -4) dung nhi 2 gay.

Ky luc gay Shin (33 tuoi) lan gan nhat vo dich la nam 2016, va co tro thanh tay golf nu dau tien trong 30 nam co khoang cach tron mot thap ky giua hai lan vo dich LPGA Tour. Nha vo dich bao ve danh hieu nguoi Anh Lottie Woad ket thuc dong hang 37.
