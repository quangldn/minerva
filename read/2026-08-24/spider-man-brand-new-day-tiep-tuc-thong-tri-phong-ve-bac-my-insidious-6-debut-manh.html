Ever since Obsession and Backrooms broke out in May, the summer movie season has been a little short on horror movies. So while The Odyssey and Spider-Man: Brand New Day have been shattering box office records, Evil Dead Burn got left in the dust. Insidious: Out of the Further hit theaters on August 21, marking the first Insidious film in three years. So how much business did the sixth installment of the massive horror franchise reel in over its first three days on the big screen?

Insidious: Out of the Further Box Office

Insidious: Out of the Further made an estimated $25.3 million at the box office between August 21 and August 23. For context, Insidious scored a $13.3 million opening back in 2011, setting the stage for Insidious Chapter 2 to post a $40.3 million opening weekend in 2013. Insidious Chapter 3 went on to open to $22.7 million in 2015, while Insidious: The Last Key opened to $29.6 million in 2018 and Insidious: The Red Door got started with $33.0 million in 2023. Adjusted for inflation, Insidious Chapter 2 owns the franchise's largest opening at $59.2 million, followed by Insidious: The Last Key at $38.8 million, Insidious: The Red Door at $36.1 million Insidious Chapter 3 at $32.2 million. The original Insidious opened to an adjusted $20.0 million.

According to Deadline, Insidious: Out of the Further made $3.0 million in domestic Thursday night previews. That cash is technically counted as part of the film's $10.6 million Friday gross. It proceeded to make an estimated $8.7 million on Saturday and $6.0 million on Sunday. Insidious: Out of the Further posted the third-largest domestic opening weekend by a horror movie so far in 2026. Backrooms opened the highest at $81.4 million, followed by Scream 7 at $63.6 million. Insidious: Out of the Further opened above Iron Lung's $18.2 million, Evil Dead Burn's $13.7 million, Lee Cronin's The Mummy's $13.5 million, 28 Years Later: The Bone Temple's $12.5 million and Primate's $11.2 million.

Looking beyond the United States and Canada, Insidious: Out of the Further has reeled in an additional $35.0 million overseas. In total, the film grossed $60.3 million worldwide over its opening weekend. After the original Insidious grossed $99.9 million globally, Insidious Chapter 2 made $161.9 million, Insidious Chapter 3 made $120.5 million, Insidious: The Last Key made $172.8 million and Insidious: The Red Door made $186.3 million.
