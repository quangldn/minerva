Our Real Madrid-Sociedad match report is live (22:07 GMT)

Missed any of tonight’s action at the Santiago Bernabeu? Real Madrid’s brilliant 4-1 win saw them brush Real Sociedad aside, courtesy of a masterful attacking display, headlined by a hat-trick from Kylian Mbappe.

Jose Mourinho’s post-match reaction (22:03 GMT)

“There’s always a sense of justice in the boos and the applause, and the odd boo now and then doesn’t do anyone any harm – it helps you grow,” Real manager Mourinho told a news conference.

“We all know the Santiago Bernabeu and just how demanding the fans are there, and I’m very pleased with how my players responded under pressure and put in a great second half, in which we could even have scored more goals.”

Valverde’s post-match thoughts (21:58 GMT)

“It’s spectacular, incredible [to have Mbappe]. We have to enjoy Mbappe and make the most of him as [much as] possible,” Los Blancos captain Federico Valverde told Real Madrid TV.

“We have to give him a thousand balls so he is comfortable, he can score goals, and he’s happy.”

Valverde said Madrid tweaked their approach to pressing in the second half and it paid dividends.

“We were finding it hard to get the ball back in their half… We adjusted that, and in the second half, we were a lot more comfortable,” explained the Uruguayan midfielder.

Champions League draw (21:48 GMT)

Following tonight’s win, Real Madrid will turn their attention to Thursday’s UEFA Champions League draw in Monaco before they face Malaga at the weekend.

Mourinho’s men find themselves in Pot One for the prestigious draw.

Also in Pot One are the likes of Liverpool, PSG, Bayern Munich and, of course, archrivals Barcelona, with each side slated to play four matches both at home and away.

Six of the best (21:35 GMT)

To add to that hat-trick stat, tonight’s marvellous display from Mbappe means he’s now scored six goals in five games against Real Sociedad in La Liga.

Poor old La Real are his favourite victim when it comes to bagging goals.

News Flash: Kylian Mbappe is good at football (21:32 GMT)

Tonight’s hat-trick for Real Madrid and France superstar Kylian Mbappe was his SIXTH in all competitions for the club.

That tally is double what any other player has been able to produce in Spain since the start of the 2024-25 season, with Alexander Sorloth (Atletico Madrid) and Raphinha (Barcelona) both with three hat-tricks.

Can Real Sociedad clinch their first win of the season? (21:20 GMT)

Real Sociedad lost their season opener in the heat of Seville as they were beaten 1-0 by Real Betis before following that up with tonight’s heavy defeat to Real Madrid.

Next up for La Real is a clash with Espanyol on Saturday at Anoeta in front of their home fans.

Is it too early to say that Rino Matarazzo’s side must win that game? I dare say the pressure will be on the team and the coach if they don’t…

Next up for Real Madrid (21:14 GMT)

They may have had a delayed start to La Liga this season, but the games are coming thick and fast for Jose Mourinho’s Real Madrid.

After last weekend’s away trip to Espanyol and tonight’s game against Real Sociedad, Madrid will play their third game in the space of eight days when they take on newly-promoted Malaga on Sunday at 5pm local time (15:00 GMT).

That game will also take place at the Santiago Bernabeu.
