Tour Championship 2026: Top 30 players who qualified for playoff finale at East Lake

Here's a look at the 30 players who qualified for the FedExCup Playoff finale at East Lake Golf Club.

The PGA Tour playoffs conclude with the Tour Championship.

The top 30 players in FEC points through the second playoff event, the BMW Championship, qualified for the finale. With FEC points reset to scratch, whoever wins at East Lake Golf Club will be declared the overall FedExCup champion.

The Tour Championship also offers an official $40 million purse with $10 million going to the winner. By qualifying for the finale, players earn a two-year PGA Tour exemption and essentially earn invites into all of the 2027 majors. They already locked up spots into next year's signature events.
