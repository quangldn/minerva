2026 CPKC Women's Open final results: Prize money payout, LPGA Tour leaderboard and how much each golfer won

By Ryan Ballengee — August 23, 2026

The 2026 CPKC Women's Open final leaderboard is headed by winner Jeeno Yamashita, who topped the LPGA Tour leaderboard this week with a win at Royal Mayfair Golf Club in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada.

Yamashita won this event for her fourth LPGA Tour win, tying the all-time LPGA Tour 72-hole scoring mark with a nine-shot win over the field on 23-under 257. Yamashita opened with 62 on Thursday and never looked back.

Yuri Yoshida finished in solo second for the week, while Sei Young Kim had another great tournament on the same week her LPGA scoring record was tied.

Yamashita won the $412,500 winner's share of the $2,750,000 purse.

CPKC Women's Open recap notes

Yamashita picks up the win in the 22nd LPGA Tour event of the season, getting in the winner's circle once again on the LPGA Tour.

By winning the event, Yamashita earned 500 points in the Race to the CME Globe, with the top 60 players in points after The Annika in November getting into the season-ending CME Group Tour Championship.

This week, there was a 36-hole cut in this event to the top 65 and ties through two rounds, with 69 players out of 156 total players finishing the tournament, including four amateurs.

The 2026 LPGA Tour schedule continues next week with the FM Championship in Massachusetts.
