Red Deer's Eileen Park tied for 154th after opening round at CPKC Women's Open

Red Deer's Eileen Park is tied for 154th after shooting eight over par in the opening round of the 2026 CPKC Women's Open. She's set to open the second round of the four-round LPGA tournament at 2:05 p.m. on Friday at the Royal Mayfair Golf Club in Edmonton.

Japanese golfer Miyu Yamashita is at the top of the leaderboard at eight under par, followed by Nasa Hataoka at seven under par. The tournament will then continue on Saturday before wrapping up on Sunday.
