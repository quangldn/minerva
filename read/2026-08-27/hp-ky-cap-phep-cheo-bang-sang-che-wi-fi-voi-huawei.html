HP to licence Huawei Wi-Fi patents

Chinese vendor Huawei and US tech firm HP have signed a multiyear global patent cross-licensing agreement, which will involve licenses to HP for certain Huawei Wi-Fi patents.

The deal will also see Huawei obtain reciprocal patent rights from HP, according to the release from Huawei – which otherwise is fairly light on details.

What it does say is that this is a milestone agreement and reflects the companies' cooperation around intellectual property licensing, and “recognizes Huawei's innovation capabilities and core technological strength as well as HP's position as global leader in computers and peripheral equipment.”

After a few paragraphs celebrating the uses of Wi-Fi technology in general, Huawei makes the point that it has played a significant role in the development of it and makes the resulting technologies available publicly, “so that innovation created in one place can benefit users everywhere.”

"Huawei is pleased to reach this patent cross-licensing agreement with HP Inc,” said Alan Fan, Huawei's Chief Intellectual Property Officer. “This agreement is a strong testament to Huawei's persistent independent innovation in cutting-edge fields in Information and Communications Technology (ICT). Through patent licensing, Huawei shares its innovation with the industry, particularly in the area of standardized technologies, which brings leading technological experiences to consumers worldwide."

Steven Geiszler, who represented Huawei in the negotiations, added: "This is another successful licensing of Huawei patents, particularly in the area of standardized Wi-Fi technologies—while obtaining valuable reciprocal patent rights from HP Inc. I appreciate the professionalism and courteousness shown by HP's negotiation team during this project."

On one level it sees like the sort of patent deal that happens all the time in tech, but there have been numerous restrictions the US has placed on doing business with Chinese firms. There’s no statement from HP included in the release, but Reuters and others quote a spokesperson as saying: "This is a standard-essential patent licence covering Wi-Fi technology. It is not new, and does not represent a broader strategic or commercial relationship, partnership, or collaboration with Huawei."

The deal today follows litigation and a subsequent agreement involving the Huawei and HP, as well as others, which resulted in HP gaining access to around 2,000 patents related to the Wi-Fi 6 standard last year.
