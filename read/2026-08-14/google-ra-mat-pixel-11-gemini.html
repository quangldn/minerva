Everything announced at Made by Google '26: Pixel 11, Pixel Watch 5, Pixel Tag, and tons of Gemini features

Google is holding its Made by Google 2026 event, unveiling the Pixel 11 series, the Pixel Watch 5, and even a competitor to Apple's AirTag. The tech giant also used the event to show off new Gemini-powered features across its devices.

One notable accessibility update is an expansion of "Live Transcribe" to support American Sign Language. Google also introduced "Rambler," a new voice-input feature designed to better understand the way people actually talk, handling run-on sentences and filler words.

The standard Pixel 11 gets a redesigned camera bar more than 40% thinner, doubled base storage to 256GB, and a starting price of $899 — a $100 increase compared to the Pixel 10, partly due to an ongoing RAM supply shortage.

Google finally has its own tracking tag, Pixel Tag, priced at $29 (or $99 for a four-pack), connecting to Android's Find Hub network.
