Europe takes lead in global 2G and 3G switch-off

The latest figures from the Global mobile Suppliers Association (GSA) indicate that Europe now accounts for 43% of all 2G/3G switch-offs globally, followed by Asia (26%), and Latin America and the Caribbean (9%).

By the end of June 2026, GSA identified 313 completed, planned or in-progress 2G and 3G switch-offs in 89 countries and territories. Some 158 operators have already completed their network closures, 119 have planned them and 36 are in progress.

Switching off legacy networks offers benefits such as refarming spectrum for 4G/5G capacity and reducing energy use. However, 2G and 3G often still support machine-to-machine and IoT connections — Orange was forced to postpone its 2G/3G sunsetting in France over concerns about elevators and other IoT systems, now aiming for 2028 and 2030.

The number of 5G subscriptions has passed the three billion mark, and half of the world's mobile data traffic is now carried over 5G, according to Ericsson's June 2026 Mobility Report.
