FS has introduced the D7070 Series Integrated 800G Muxponder, extending its DCI portfolio from 100G through 800G with 3.2 Tb/s of coherent line capacity for data center, metro and regional networks.

FS is extending its Data Center Interconnect (DCI) portfolio to 800G with the introduction of the D7070 Series Integrated 800G Muxponder, targeting growing inter-site bandwidth requirements driven by AI infrastructure and cloud networks. The 1U platform aggregates 100GbE and 400GbE client services onto four 800G coherent wavelengths, providing up to 3.2 Tb/s of aggregate line capacity for data center, metro and regional network applications.

The D7070 supports 100G QSFP28 and 400G QSFP112 client-side pluggables together with 800G CFP2-DCO coherent line modules. FS is pairing the platform with its 800G ZR tunable DWDM CFP2-DCO coherent module, providing a path for operators to move higher-capacity traffic onto DWDM infrastructure while retaining lower-rate client interfaces. The system also uses an open software architecture with WebGUI and CLI management interfaces and is designed to integrate with third-party network management systems.

The new platform sits at the high end of a broader FS DCI portfolio spanning 100G through 800G. The D5110 Series targets 100G point-to-point links up to 120 km; the D710 Series provides 200G OTN transport with AES-256 Layer-1 encryption; the D6000 Series supports compact 400G ZR/ZR+ deployments; and the D7000 Series addresses 400G metro and regional transport with capacity of up to 19.2 Tb/s per fiber pair. FS said its longer-term roadmap includes higher-speed transmission, intelligent optical line systems and centralized control of the DCI portfolio through its AmpCon management software.

Scale Across architectures increasingly require high-capacity optical links between geographically separated clusters, making DCI an important extension of the AI networking fabric rather than simply a conventional cloud connectivity application.

The choice of an 800G coherent pluggable line interface is also significant. The OIF's 800ZR specification defines 800G coherent connectivity for single-span amplified DWDM links of approximately 80–120 km, extending the architectural model established by 400ZR. The industry is simultaneously pushing ZR+ implementations toward regional applications, creating a continuum between router-based coherent pluggables and dedicated optical transport platforms.

For FS, the D7070 broadens an optical portfolio that increasingly overlaps multiple layers of AI infrastructure. The company has previously introduced 400G/800G Ethernet switching, 800G LPO modules, high-density optical connectivity and optical circuit switching. Adding an 800G muxponder gives FS another component for the inter-data-center portion of the network, while its stated AmpCon roadmap points toward unified management across these optical transport systems.
