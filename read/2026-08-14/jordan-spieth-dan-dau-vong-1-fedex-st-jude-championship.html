Wellesley's Michael Thorbjornsen, Jordan Spieth part of five-way tie for lead in PGA Tour playoff opener

Jordan Spieth was bogey-free for a 5-under 65 that gave him a five-way share of the lead in the FedEx St. Jude Championship. Rocket Classic champion Michael Thorbjornsen, Jake Knapp, Michael Kim, and Kurt Kitayama also opened with 65s at TPC Southwind in Memphis.

Seven players, including defending FedEx Cup champion Tommy Fleetwood, Hideki Matsuyama, Gary Woodland, and Adam Scott were at 66. World No. 1 Scottie Scheffler opened with a 68, while Rory McIlroy finished bogey-bogey-double bogey for 74.
