UEFA Champions League draw results: League phase foes set on the road to 2027 Madrid final

The road to the 2026-27 UEFA Champions League final in Madrid was paved on Thursday, as the draw for the 36-team league phase took place in Monaco.

Arsenal, Manchester City, Manchester United, Aston Villa and Liverpool have qualified from the Premier League — in that order last season — with dreams of lifting club football's most prestigious trophy inside Atletico Madrid's Estadio Metropolitano on June 5.

Below are the league phase foes for the top teams in this year's Champions League. (Teams in order based on UEFA club coefficients. First row is opponent from Pot 1, second row from Pot 2, and so on.)

PSG — Home: Barcelona, Roma, Galatasaray, Slovan Bratislava. Away: Manchester City, Aston Villa, Villarreal, Como.

Bayern Munich — Home: Arsenal, Real Betis, Bodo/Glimt, Slavia Prague. Away: Atletico Madrid, Manchester United, Lille, Viking.

Real Madrid — Home: Inter Milan, PSV, RB Leipzig, LASK. Away: Arsenal, Roma, Shakhtar Donetsk, AEK Athens.

Liverpool — Home: Atletico Madrid, Porto, Villarreal, Lens. Away: Inter Milan, Club Brugge, Fenerbahce, LASK.

Inter Milan — Home: Liverpool, Club Brugge, Shakhtar Donetsk, Stuttgart. Away: Real Madrid, Borussia Dortmund, Feyenoord, Slovan Bratislava.

Manchester City — Home: PSG, Sporting CP, Napoli, AEK Athens. Away: Barcelona, Porto, RB Leipzig, Lens.

Arsenal — Home: Real Madrid, Borussia Dortmund, Lille, Sabah. Away: Bayern Munich, Real Betis, Napoli, Slavia Prague.

Barcelona — Home: Manchester City, Aston Villa, Feyenoord, Como. Away: PSG, Sporting CP, Galatasaray, Sabah.

Atletico Madrid — Home: Bayern Munich, Manchester United, Fenerbahce, Viking. Away: Liverpool, PSV, Bodo/Glimt, Stuttgart.

Borussia Dortmund — Home: Inter Milan, Real Betis, Villarreal, AEK Athens. Away: Arsenal, Aston Villa, Bodo/Glimt, Sabah.

Aston Villa — Home: PSG, Borussia Dortmund, Fenerbahce, Viking. Away: Barcelona, Club Brugge, Galatasaray, Slavia Prague.

Porto — Home: Manchester City, PSV, Napoli, Slavia Prague. Away: Liverpool, Real Betis, Feyenoord, LASK.
