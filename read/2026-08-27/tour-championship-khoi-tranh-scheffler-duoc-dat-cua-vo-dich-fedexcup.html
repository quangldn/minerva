2026 Tour Championship Preview, Full Field And Odds

By Jeff Hartman — Aug 26, 2026. Condensed summary of the Forbes preview (full text not reproduced for copyright reasons).

The Tour Championship, the third and final leg of the PGA Tour's FedEx Cup Playoffs, begins Thursday. It follows the St. Jude Championship and the BMW Championship, and narrows the postseason field to the top 30 players.

Tournament details

Dates: Aug 27-30, 2026. Location: Atlanta, Georgia. Course: East Lake Golf Club (Par 70, 7,440 yards). Field: 30 players. Total purse: $40 million.

Recent winners

2025 – Tommy Fleetwood; 2024 – Scottie Scheffler; 2023 – Viktor Hovland; 2022 – Rory McIlroy; 2021 – Patrick Cantlay.

Pre-tournament winner odds

Scottie Scheffler (+340); Rory McIlroy (+810); Xander Schauffele (+1275); Ludvig Aberg (+1375); Sam Burns (+1475).

Picks

Favourite pick: Scottie Scheffler (+340). Long-shot pick: Viktor Hovland (+2600). The writer notes Scheffler finished T12 the previous week while ill with hand, foot and mouth disease, and expects him to contend at East Lake now that he is healthy; Hovland, the 2023 champion, is flagged as the value play among longer odds.

Top 5 bets: Wyndham Clark (+240) and Rory McIlroy (+132) — the writer argues the books are undervaluing Clark's season, and that McIlroy's rebound at the BMW Championship makes positive odds attractive in a 30-man field.

Top 10 picks: Collin Morikawa (+108) and Viktor Hovland (+130).

Top 20: no plays, since a 30-golfer field leaves no value without a parlay.

Bonus plays: Double chance — Scottie Scheffler or Si Woo Kim (+280); Triple chance — Scheffler / Schauffele / Tom Kim (+225); Margin of victory — 2 shots (+360).
