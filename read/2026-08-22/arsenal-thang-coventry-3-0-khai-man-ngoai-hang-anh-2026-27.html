Arsenal thrash promoted Coventry to kick off the Premier League!

Arsenal get off to a flying start! On Friday evening, in the opening match of the 2026/27 Premier League season, Arsenal made short work of newly promoted Coventry City (3-0).

That goal was very quickly followed by one from Emirates favorite Bukayo Saka, who took advantage of a mix-up in the Coventry defense after good work from Christos Tzolis (23rd). In the second half, captain Martin Odegaard finally put an end to any suspense by scoring the goal to make it 3-0 (49th).

A few days after winning the Community Shield against Manchester City, the Gunners have therefore sent a very clear message to their rivals at the start of the league campaign. For their part, Coventry City were immediately confronted with the harsh reality of top-level football in their first Premier League match in 25 years.
