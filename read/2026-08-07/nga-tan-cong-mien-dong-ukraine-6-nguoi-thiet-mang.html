Russian strikes kill six in Ukraine as Moscow intercepts hundreds of drones

Ukrainian officials say three people killed in Kharkiv region and three in Sumy region, as Russia says it intercepted 605 drones. Published 6 Aug 2026, Al Jazeera Staff, AFP and Reuters.

Russian strikes on eastern Ukraine have killed at least six people overnight, local authorities have said, as Moscow shot down 605 Ukrainian drones over its territory and the annexed Crimean Peninsula.

Rescuers worked on Thursday to douse fires and clear debris in Balakliya, in the eastern Kharkiv region, after Russian strikes on homes killed three people, authorities said on the Telegram messaging app. Vitalii Karabanov, head of the military administration in the front-line city, said the victims were two women and a man.

Three more people were killed overnight and at least 19 others wounded in Russian attacks in the northern region of Sumy. Twelve people were wounded in the city of Sumy following an attack with guided aerial bombs, according to Ukrainian police. Separately, two people were wounded in a Russian attack in the Dnipropetrovsk region, and a drone attack on a van wounded six people in the southern region of Kherson.

In Ukraine's Black Sea area, a Russian attack damaged a foreign-flagged ship loaded with wheat, killing a crew member and causing a fire, according to Odesa regional governor Oleh Kiper. Ukraine's seaport administration said the vessel Mera Queen, under the flag of Guinea-Bissau, was hit late on Wednesday. For more than a month, Russia has been ramping up its efforts to strangle Ukraine's trade and export routes, launching near-daily attacks on the country's Black Sea ports.

Meanwhile, Russia's Ministry of Defence said its forces had shot down 605 Ukrainian drones overnight. Local authorities reported that a logistics centre belonging to Russian e-commerce giant Wildberries had been "slightly damaged." At least 20 warehouses owned by the company have been attacked since July 18; Kyiv alleges the retailer plays a role in supplying dual-use items for Moscow's war effort.

Later on Thursday, Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the military hit two oil refineries deep inside Russia — the Bashneft-Novoil refinery in Bashkortostan and the Slavneft-Yaroslavl refinery in the Yaroslavl region, which caught fire after the drone attack.
