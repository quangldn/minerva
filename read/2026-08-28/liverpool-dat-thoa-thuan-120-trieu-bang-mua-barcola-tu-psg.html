Bradley Barcola transfer news: Liverpool reach £120m deal in principle for PSG forward

Liverpool have reached a £120m deal in principle to sign PSG forward Bradley Barcola; deal not finalised yet but major breakthrough in talks; there has been a gap in valuation between two clubs throughout summer, with PSG asking for around £145m for Barcola.

Thursday 27 August 2026 09:15, UK

Liverpool have an agreement in principle with Paris Saint-Germain to sign Bradley Barcola for a package worth £120m.

The deal has not been finalised, with last details still being discussed, but there has been a breakthrough in negotiations. There had been a gap in valuation between the two clubs throughout the summer, with PSG previously asking for around £145m for the France international.

Winger Bradley Barcola has been a Liverpool target all summer.

Liverpool are also trying to sign a second new winger, with their target currently Crystal Palace's Ismaila Sarr following difficult negotiations with Brighton for Yankuba Minteh. Liverpool have approached Palace over a potential deal for Sarr, but Palace do not want to let Sarr go at this stage and consider the 28-year-old to be not for sale. He has three years left on his contract.

Sarr missed Palace's opening weekend defeat at Everton. Reports claimed he was refusing to train but it is understood that the winger is currently injured.

Liverpool are also willing to let Cody Gakpo leave Anfield, with Tottenham and Manchester City both interested in the Dutchman.
