France, Germany, UK and Italy condemn Israel's 'E1' West Bank plan — Reuters. Published: 20 August, 2026: 07:48 PM GST. Updated: 20 August, 2026: 08:50 PM GST

A drone view shows the area planned to be developed for the E1 settlement, which will displace several Bedouin communities, in Jabal Al-Baba, in the Israeli-occupied West Bank, on September 17, 2025. (Reuters)

France, Germany, Britain and Italy condemned on Thursday plans by Israel to open up construction tenders for a residential property project called "E1" in the occupied West Bank.

"The decision by the Israeli government to publish bid offers for construction projects within the framework of the E1 settlers' project is unacceptable," said a joint statement by the leaders of those four countries.

Hundreds of thousands of Israelis have settled among millions of Palestinians in land captured by Israel in a 1967 war. Nearly all countries and a range of UN bodies consider that such settlements violate international law, although Israel disputes this, citing historical and biblical ties to the land.

Bối cảnh (theo Just Security, Early Edition 20/08/2026 và BBC News 19/08/2026):

Israel has opened up tenders for the construction of around 1,200 settlement homes in the so-called E1 area of the occupied West Bank. U.K. Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband condemned the publication of the tender as "an unacceptable and destructive act," and called on Israel to "halt E1 plans immediately, retract the tender and stop all settlement expansion."

Miliband said "E1 would cut across the heart of Palestine and risks separating the West Bank from East Jerusalem, which would endanger the viability of a two-state solution." Britain summoned Israel's charge d'affaires over the tender, demanding its immediate withdrawal.
