Nebius to lease data center capacity from Vantage in Newport, Wales

AI cloud firm Nebius is to deploy capacity at Vantage Data Centers' campus in Wales. The companies this week announced an agreement to deploy high-density, Nvidia-powered AI infrastructure at Vantage's CWL1 campus in Newport.

Under the agreement, Nebius will lease high-density capacity at CWL1 to support AI training, inference, agentic AI, and enterprise AI workloads. The size of the deployment wasn't shared, but will be based on Nvidia's DSX AI factory reference design.

"This is an important milestone for South Wales and for the UK's AI infrastructure ambitions," said David Howson, president, EMEA, Vantage Data Centers.

The company is expanding its footprint across Wales, and says it aims to deploy 1GW of AI-ready capacity across sites in Newport, Bridgend, and outside Cardiff in Bro Tathan.
