Tai bang B ASEAN Cup 2026 (ASEAN Hyundai Cup) tren san Thuwunna o Yangon, Malaysia nguoc dong danh bai chu nha Myanmar 2-1 de khoi dau thang loi.

Myat Kaung Khant mo ty so cho Myanmar o phut 10, nhung Paulo Josue toa sang trong hiep hai voi cu dup chi trong 5 phut: go hoa o phut 52 bang cu xoay nguoi dut diem va an dinh chien thang o phut 57 tren cham phat den.

Josue duoc bau chon la Cau thu xuat sac nhat tran (Hyundai Player of the Match). Ket qua giup thay tro HLV Tan Cheng Hoe co tron 3 diem ra quan.
