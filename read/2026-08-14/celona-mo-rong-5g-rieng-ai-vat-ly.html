Celona expands private 5G to meet the rise of physical AI

Private wireless network specialist Celona is expanding from its 5G cellular roots with a new offering that includes Wi-Fi, satellite and public network support along with agentic AI to manage it all. The proposition, called Orion, is described as "an agentic converged wireless platform" and will be available in the fourth quarter of this year.

For the first time, Celona is adding Wi-Fi to its traditional private 5G solution, offering Wi-Fi 7 access points along with 5G radios. "With this, we're addressing that and saying, you don't have to make this false choice ... and our orchestrator can manage the networks together," said CEO Rajeev Shah.

For enterprises with Starlink connectivity, the Orion orchestrator can now also manage these links alongside 5G and Wi-Fi. The orchestrator's new Orchestrator AI, based on Celona's own model "Celona Brain," creates a library of skills and agents — "digital teammates" for enterprise wireless teams.

Most of Celona's enterprise customers are in manufacturing, logistics, mining, ports, retail and transportation. "Every customer I've spoken to, the new robots they're getting from their vendors have GPUs and are running inference models on them," said Shah.
