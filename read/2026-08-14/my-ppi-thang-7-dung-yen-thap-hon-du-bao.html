Stock Market Today (Aug. 13, 2026): PPI tháng 7 đứng yên, thấp hơn dự báo

The July Producer Price Index (PPI) was unchanged on a seasonally adjusted basis, missing the Dow Jones consensus forecast of a 0.2% increase, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported.

The July Consumer Price Index, released Wednesday (Aug 12), increased 0.1% month over month, matching expectations. Bank of America economist Stephen Juneau said the report "pointed to a cooling trajectory for inflation."

Oil prices fell amid demand concerns: WTI crude dropped 1.84% to $81.74 per barrel, Brent crude fell 1.80% to $87.38 per barrel, as the IEA projected global oil demand would contract by 1.6 million barrels per day in 2026.
