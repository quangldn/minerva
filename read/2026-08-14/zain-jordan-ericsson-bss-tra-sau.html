Zain Jordan picks Ericsson to boost BSS

Zain Jordan's business support system is getting an upgrade with Ericsson at the helm and Red Hat OpenShift for cloud-native billing, extending the scope of work to Zain's postpaid business line.

"This next phase of our BSS transformation with Ericsson marks a strategic milestone in Zain Jordan's ongoing digital evolution," said Wessam Abu Hashhash, Chief Technology and Digital Innovation Officer at Zain Jordan.

Gaining real-time data insights is another aspect of the work which includes the deployment of Ericsson's Telco DataOps Platform, enabling real-time streaming, proactive customer experience management, network optimization, and establishing the basis for AI-driven autonomous networks.
