Openreach plans XGS-PON expansion

Telecoms.com · Aug. 19, 2026

UK fixed line wholesaler Openreach is planning to upgrade more of its network with XGS-PON technology, following a trial in March.

Openreach says the new technology can go beyond the top download speeds of 1.8Gbps on full fibre and push up to 8.5Gbps - which is apparently 28 times faster than the download speed a typical UK household.

Openreach plans to make these speeds available to more than a million premises over the next year, after launching an ongoing XGS-PON pilot in March covering 40,000 residential premises in Guildford and surrounding areas.

In the long term the firm is looking to cover around a third of its full fibre network with the technology over the next five years, through a programme of further build, network upgrades and equipment modernisation.

It will continue to review further expansion opportunities based on market demand, and feedback from its provider customers, we're told.

Openreach boasts its full fibre network reaches more than 23.4m premises at latest count - and is built largely using GPON technology. "But as more people move onto fibre and demand for data continues to grow, newer technology is needed to unlock even higher speeds and additional network capacity," it claims.

"Our Full Fibre network already offers Gigabit capable broadband to more than 23.4 million premises, and more than 9.4 million customers have upgraded so far," said Trevor Linney, Openreach's Director of Network Technology. "As demand for data continues to grow, our investment in XGS-PON technology will enable us to offer even higher speeds over the same network, and the additional capacity needed to support the UK's digital future.

"By continuing to invest in upgrades, we're delivering a platform that will power the next generation of technology, from AI-enabled services, smart homes and future mobile networks, to cloud-based business and public services."

On the retail side EE started flogging a fibre product in Guilford and Woking last month based on Openreach's XGS-PON, with the operator branding it as Advanced Full Fibre. The 2.3Gbps plan was priced at £54.99 per month, and the 8Gbps plan for £74.99 per month. EE said it had capacity to support up to 190 connected devices simultaneously.

Rival CityFibre announced its own 8.5Gb product on its 10Gb XGS-PON network in March, enabling its customers "to provide game changing services that power innovation and growth for homes, businesses and the UK economy," boasted the release at the time.
