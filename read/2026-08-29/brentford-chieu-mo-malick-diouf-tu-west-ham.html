Brentford sign El Hadji Malick Diouf from West Ham United

Rian Mullaley, VAVEL — August 28, 2026

Brentford make their fifth first team addition of the window, as left back El Hadji Malick Diouf joins from West Ham United.

Brentford have completed the signing of Senegalese international full back El Hadji Malick Diouf from West Ham United.

The 21-year-old becomes the Bees' fifth first team signing of the summer transfer window, after Jannik Schuster, Jaidon Anthony, Callum Wilson and Mamadou Sangare, joining for an undisclosed fee believed to be in the region of £35 million plus £5 million add ons.

An AFCON winner with Senegal in January, Diouf joins Keith Andrews' side off the back of a season in which he recorded five assists in 32 Premier League appearances with West Ham, and is expected to add great attacking threat to a side which has already scored nine goals in its opening two games of the season.

What can Diouf bring to Brentford?

El Hadji Malick Diouf's tally of five assists in the Premier League last season was the second highest of any full back in the Premier League, and higher than that of any player in the Brentford squad, with Mikkel Damsgaard and Vitaly Janelt registering the team's joint highest individual assist tally of four each.

It is evident that Brentford's transfer business this summer has, in part, made an effort to improve creativity within the team.

Club record signing Mamadou Sangare, who joined the West Londoners from Ligue 1 side RC Lens this summer, assisted Brentford's first goal of the season in their 3-0 win against Tottenham Hotspur on the opening weekend of the Premier League, before picking up another assist off the bench in the 6-1 win over Birmingham City in the Carabao Cup.

Meanwhile Jaidon Anthony, who also joined Brentford this summer from Burnley, picked up three excellent assists on full debut in the cup win at St Andrew's.

In his short but impressive career so far, Diouf has gained a growing reputation for his deadly crossing ability. The left back recorded a 28.2% accuracy rate with crosses last season, the fourth best from a full back in the Premier League. Furthermore, an average of 2.14 balls over the top per 90 ranked Diouf 7th amongst Premier League full backs.

With a front 3 of Kevin Schade, Igor Thiago and Dango Ouattara, Brentford have a front three well equipped to benefit from Diouf's strengths, holding both the pace to get in behind from balls over the top, and the aerial ability to get on the end of, and convert, good crosses into the box.

Although Diouf did not score in his 36 appearances for West Ham, his time with Slavia Prague suggests the Senegalese can also provide a goalscoring threat from defence if utilised correctly, scoring nine goals in 50 games for the Czech side.

Defensive concerns

Statistically, the defensive profile of Diouf indicates a player with elite aerial ability for a full back and a relatively impressive reading of the game, with room for improvement when it comes to positioning and engagement with opposition attackers.

The 183cm frame of Diouf is reasonably tall for a full back and is reflected in his impressive aerial ability compared to most full backs, averaging 1.49 aerial duels won per 90 at a success rate of 55%, ranking him in the 80th percentile of full backs in the Premier League last season.

An average of 1.10 interceptions with 4.18 ball recoveries per 90 saw Diouf ranked in the high 50 percentiles amongst Premier League full backs for both, whilst his average of 0.43 blocked shots, 3.85 clearances and 0.46 times dribbled past per 90 were all in the high 70 to low 80 percentiles.

The clear weakness, and most likely the reason for defensive criticisms, comes from tackling. Whilst his tackle success rate of 46% and ground duels success rate of 53% rank respectably amongst Premier League full backs, Diouf's average of 1.46 tackles per 90 ranked him in the 15th percentile.

What does this mean for Brentford's other full backs?

At £35 million, potentially rising to £40 million, the signing of El Hadji Malick Diouf comes in as Brentford's third most expensive of all time, behind only Mamadou Sangare and Dango Ouattara. As a result, it can definitely be implied that Brentford's new man is viewed as a serious starting contender.

The arrival of Diouf ensures that the Bees have five left back options in their current squad. 20-year-old Jayden Meghoma, who spent last season on loan at Rangers, is expected to go out on loan again.

The biggest talking point is that of Rico Henry. The Jamaican international has spent 10 years in West London, playing 240 games and no doubt reaching the status of a Brentford legend. However, he lacks the versatility of Lewis-Potter and Hickey, and at 29-years-old does not possess the youth and potential of Meghoma or Diouf — which could potentially force him to reconsider his future ahead of Tuesday's deadline.
