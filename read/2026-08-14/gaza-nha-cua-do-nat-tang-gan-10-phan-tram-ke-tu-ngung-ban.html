Gaza: Destruction of buildings rises by nearly 10 per cent since ceasefire

New satellite assessments show the destruction of buildings in Gaza has climbed by nearly 10 per cent since October's faltering ceasefire between Hamas and Israeli forces, UN agencies said on Thursday. Even with the guns largely silent, Palestinians in Gaza continue to face daily threats from unexploded ordnance left behind by the fighting.

At least three Israeli airstrikes have been reported since Wednesday, including one near Al Amal Hospital in Khan Younis earlier on Thursday. A 13-year-old was injured by explosive ordnance earlier this week in northern Gaza.

Overall, about 82 per cent of the structures – about 200,000 buildings – in Gaza are damaged, with two thirds of them destroyed, UNRWA said. UN agencies are also facing mounting shortages of fuel and other essential items that strain service delivery.
