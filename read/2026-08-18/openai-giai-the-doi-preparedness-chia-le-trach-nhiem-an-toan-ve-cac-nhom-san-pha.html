OpenAI reportedly disbanded its preparedness team — the latest shakeup of its safety organization as the company heads toward an IPO. By Terrence O'Brien, The Verge, Aug 17, 2026.

Citing the Financial Times, The Verge reports that OpenAI shut down its preparedness team at the end of last month. The group's remit had been to evaluate whether new models posed serious risks — including scenarios such as a model going rogue and attacking another company's systems — and to design mitigations for them. Rather than keeping that work in one dedicated unit, the FT says responsibility has been split by risk domain, with areas such as biological and cyber threats folded into existing product and research teams.

The move continues a multi-year pattern at OpenAI, which has already wound down its AGI readiness and superalignment groups. Several senior figures have departed recently as well, including ethics lead Chloe Bakalar, chief futurist Josh Achiam, and head of safety Johannes Heidecke. Jan Leike, who resigned from OpenAI in 2024, told the FT that the company was deprioritizing safety in favor of building what he called "shiny products."

Dylan Scandinaro, who led the preparedness team after being recruited from Anthropic in February, will now concentrate on the implications of recursively self-improving AI. The reorganization comes amid broader internal upheaval as OpenAI moves toward what is expected to be a very large public offering.
