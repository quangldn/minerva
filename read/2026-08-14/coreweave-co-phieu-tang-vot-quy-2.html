CoreWeave surge bolsters the case for the AI trade

CoreWeave's losses doubled, its spending soared and it devoured almost $6 billion more in cash than it generated last quarter. Investors loved it.

Why it matters: Some see CoreWeave, a so-called neocloud that builds data centers and sells AI computing power, as the canary in the coal mine of the AI trade.

Shares of the neocloud soared 19% after it reported second-quarter results. Revenue more than doubled, to $2.58 billion. Remaining performance obligations, a measure of sales backlog, jumped 245% to $103.7 billion.

Yes, but: the company's losses are getting deeper for every dollar of sales it collects. Its adjusted net loss worsened to $567 million from $130 million during the same quarter last year, and free cash flow was negative $5.74 billion, a sharp deterioration from negative $2.70 billion the previous year.
