Trump White House says it's losing $19B-$26B a year in revenue as countries dodge tariffs

WASHINGTON — The Trump White House said in a new report on Thursday that countries are routing their exports through third countries to avoid U.S. tariffs, estimating tax revenue losses of $19 billion to $26 billion annually.

The report highlights that China responded to tariffs by sending goods to other nations ranging from Mexico to Malaysia for packaging and limited assembly — a practice known as transshipping. Peter Navarro, the White House trade adviser, said China is laundering its exports through more than 40 countries.

The report comes ahead of a planned September visit by Chinese Leader Xi Jinping. To address the challenge, Navarro said U.S. Customs and Border Protection has started using artificial intelligence in a prototype program to stop transshipments.
