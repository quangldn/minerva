Sunil Sadani and Dmitry Shokarev discuss how AI is transforming network infrastructure requirements and the routing solutions designed to meet these demands. The conversation explores the evolution from AI training to AI consumption, and the critical role of data center interconnect in supporting distributed GPU clusters.

The presenters examine the PTX router portfolio and its Express silicon architecture, revealing how custom-built ASICs address the unique challenges of AI-era networking. They detail the technical capabilities that enable massive data movement between data centers and discuss the integration of coherent optics for simplified connectivity.

Key points covered

How scale-up, scale-out, and scale-across architectures support different AI deployment models.

The technical specifications and capabilities of the PTX 12000 Series routers.

How Express 5 silicon delivers industry-leading 800G port density.

The role of integrated ZR optics in eliminating traditional optical line systems.

Built-in security features including line-rate MACsec encryption.

The roadmap for 1.6TB and 3.2TB interface evolution.

How custom silicon design addresses AI workload requirements.
