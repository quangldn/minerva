Iran Battles Oil Spill Near Key Persian Gulf Island of Qeshm

Iran is battling an oil spill off Qeshm Island, underscoring the environmental risks posed by attacks on tankers in the Persian Gulf. Satellite images show a dark, elongated trail in waters off the coastal village of Shib Deraz — a breeding ground for endangered hawksbill sea turtles — as well as black deposits on the beaches.

The risk of spills in the Gulf has increased with multiple assaults on tankers during the five-month US-Iran war. The International Maritime Organization says there have been 65 confirmed incidents in the Strait of Hormuz and across the Middle East since the beginning of March.

TankerTrackers.com suggested the spill resulted from an Iranian attack on a bulk carrier earlier this month. The reported spill comes just as Oman responds to a separate oil slick near its southeastern coast after a tanker ran aground.
